# Dynamic Earth, Edinburgh · 3D site model

A detailed, true-to-scale 3D model of Dynamic Earth on Holyrood Road, Edinburgh, and the ground 50 m around the whole site (a 205 × 219 m block, cut out like an architect's site model). The fabric roof, its masts, cables and glazed trusses, the glass hall, the stone terrace, the amphitheatre and its stage are built from Scottish LiDAR, the architects' published plan and photographs. The surroundings, including the south side of the Scottish Parliament, come from LiDAR and OpenStreetMap
